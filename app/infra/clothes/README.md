# Garment models (plan 19)

3D clothes from the MakeHuman Community asset packs (Shirts 01, Shirts 02, Pants 01, Skirts 01, Dress 01, Shoes 01, Shoes 02, Suits 01, Hats 01/03, Glasses 01/02, Gloves 01,
Equipment 03, Underwear 04;
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html). CC0 and CC-BY (3.0/4.0)
(attribution required: keep the table below; checked against each pack's catalog; files whose own header says AGPL
were left out, even when the catalog says otherwise). Jackets, blazers, the tie and the bow tie are cut out of the
Suits 01 suits by `split_parts.py` (run it again after changing `PARTS`). A texture PIL can't read is skipped (the
garment then has no shading detail). Kept: the `.mhclo` binding, the `.obj` mesh, the `.mhmat`
material and its diffuse texture shrunk to 1024 px (the renderer only uses it for shading detail; the
colour comes from the user's photo). Normal/spec/height maps were dropped.
